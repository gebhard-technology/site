Privacy Policy: Little Photos

Last updated 27 November 2026

Little Photos is a gentle photo book for young children, for iPhone and iPad. It shows photos from an album you choose, one to a page. This policy explains what it does with information. The short version: it collects nothing, and nothing about you or your photos is sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Little Photos has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

Your photos

The app reads photos through Apple’s Photos framework, only from the albums you choose, and only after you have given it permission. You can give it access to your whole library or to just the photos you pick.

  • Photos are never copied out of your photo library, and never uploaded.
  • Hiding a photo in the app only stops the book from showing it. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in Photos.
  • If you tap “Make a new album for me”, the app creates one empty album named “Little Photos” in your photo library. That is the only thing it ever writes to Photos, and only when you ask for it.

You can change or withdraw photo access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of its own settings on your device: which albums are on the shelf, which one is open, which photos you have hidden, the page each album was left on, whether pages turn by themselves, and whether setup is finished. Albums and photos are recorded as the identifiers iOS uses for them, not as copies of the photos themselves.

That information never leaves your device except as part of your own device backup, which is controlled entirely by your device settings and is not accessible to us: on iPhone and iPad, iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup.

Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • There are no third-party SDKs that collect data, show advertising, or track you. The app is built only from Apple’s own frameworks.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own, and has no servers to connect to. When a photo in the album you chose is kept only in iCloud, the app asks iOS to fetch that photo so it can be shown clearly rather than blurred. iOS makes that request, to your own iCloud account, for your own photo. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing about you or your photos is sent anywhere else.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Children's privacy

Little Photos is designed for families. It is listed in the App Store Kids Category. Because the app collects no data and sends nothing about you or your photos off your device, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the App Store’s Kids Category requirements by design.

The app asks children for nothing at all: no name, no age, and no sign-in. The screens with words on them are for a grown-up, behind a press-and-hold gate; the only words a child might see are “press and hold”, shown for a moment when the gear is tapped.

Links out of the app

The app contains no links to external websites, no social media features, and no way to contact anyone from inside the app.
