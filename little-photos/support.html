Common questions

How does my child use it?

Tap an album on the shelf to open it. Each page shows one photo. To turn the page, tap the curled corner at the bottom right; it lifts a little under the finger so it's easy to find. Tapping the photo itself never turns the page. When the last photo has been seen, the book closes, so “all done” is easy to see. Turn the phone or iPad sideways to see two pages at once.

How do I get to the grown-up settings?

Press and hold the small gear in the top right corner for about a second. A quick tap only shows the words “press and hold”, so little fingers stay in the book. From the grown-up screen you can:

  • Add an album to the shelf, up to eight, or take one off.
  • Hide a photo so the book skips it. It stays in Photos.
  • Turn pages by themselves every 5, 10 or 20 seconds. This is off unless you turn it on.
  • See how photo access is set, and add more photos if you chose Limit Access.

Which photos can it show?

Only the albums you put on the shelf: Favorites, any album from Photos, or a new empty “Little Photos” album the app can make for you. With Full Access, a photo you add to that album in Photos appears in the book on its own.

What do the three photo access choices mean?

  • Allow Full Access (recommended): photos you add to the album later show up in the book on their own. The app still only shows the albums you choose.
  • Limit Access: you pick the exact photos the app can see. New photos won't appear until you add them from the grown-up screen.
  • Don't Allow: the book can't show any photos.

I chose Don't Allow. How do I turn photo access on?

Open the Settings app, tap Apps, tap Little Photos, tap Photos, then choose Full Access. Come back to Little Photos and it picks up where setup left off.

Why does a photo look blurry for a moment?

If your iPhone or iPad keeps full-size photos in iCloud to save space, iOS fetches the sharp copy of that photo from your own iCloud when it's needed. It's shown softly until it arrives.

Does it remember where we left off?

Yes. Each album remembers its page, even if you switch albums or close the app. Once the book has been read to the end, it starts again from the first page next time.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Little Photos is for iPhone and iPad for now.

Where is my information kept?

On your device only. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it never copies or uploads your photos. See the privacy policy for the detail.
