Their people, one page at a time

Little ones love looking at photos of the people and places they know, but a whole photo library is a lot of swiping, and one wrong tap lands somewhere else. I made Little Photos so my kids could flip through the pictures I chose for them, like a real album on the coffee table, and nothing more.

Up to eight albums on the shelf, each in its own cloth.

  • You choose the photosPick Favorites or any album from Photos. Add a photo to that album later and it shows up in the book on its own.
  • One photo to a pageEach photo is mounted on a painted album page, with nothing else on the screen.
  • Turn the pageLittle fingers tap the curled corner to turn. No swiping, no menus, and nowhere else to go.
  • A clear endingThe last page closes the book, so “all done” is easy to see.
  • A shelf of albumsPut up to eight albums on the shelf. The book remembers the page each one was left on.
  • Grown-ups onlyPress and hold the little gear to add albums, hide a photo, or let pages turn by themselves.
  • Turn it sidewaysOn a phone or iPad turned sideways, the album opens out to two pages at once.
  • Calm by designNo sounds, no timers, no rewards. Just slow, soft page turns.

Your photos stay yours

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. The app only shows the album you choose, never copies or uploads a photo, and nothing about you or your photos ever leaves your device.
