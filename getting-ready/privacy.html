Getting Ready Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Getting Ready is a picture routine chart for children, available for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what the app does with information on both. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Getting Ready has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The app saves one file on your device containing your charts: for each child, the tasks you have chosen, which flaps are currently lifted, the colour you picked, and their first name if you have entered one; and the date the flaps were last reset.

That file never leaves your device except as part of your own device backup, which is controlled entirely by your device settings and is not accessible to us:

  • On iPhone, iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup.
  • On Android, your Android backup to your Google account, or a transfer when you move to a new phone.

If that file is ever damaged, the app keeps a copy of the damaged file next to it on the device, so a chart can be recovered rather than lost. That copy is never backed up or sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes these files.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • There are no third-party SDKs that collect data, show advertising, or track you. The app is built only from standard platform libraries (on Android, Google's AndroidX and Kotlin libraries), which send nothing anywhere.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network connections.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Children's privacy

Getting Ready is designed for families. It is listed in the App Store Kids Category and is built to meet the Google Play Families policy. Because the app collects no data and makes no network connections, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the App Store's Kids Category requirements and the Google Play Families policy by design.

Each child's first name, if entered, is optional. It is used only to label their chart and to display a message when a routine is finished, and it is stored only on the device and in your own device backup described above.

Links out of the app

The app contains no links to external websites, no social media features, and no way to contact anyone from inside the app.
