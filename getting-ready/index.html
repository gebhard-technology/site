From one mom to the next

I made Getting Ready for my own kids, for the mornings and bedtimes when everything feels like a struggle. Instead of me repeating “shoes on!” ten times, they follow their picture chart and lift a flap for each step. It helped us on the hard days, so I wanted to share it with you.

  • Lift a flap for each stepBrush teeth, get dressed, shoes on. Every finished task shows a cheerful picture.
  • Morning and nightA double-sided board. Tap the sun or moon to flip to the other routine.
  • Your own tasksPick from ready-made tasks or make your own.
  • More than one childEach child gets their own chart and their own color.
  • Fresh every dayThe flaps close on their own each new day.
  • Prop it upWorks sideways too, so it can stand on the counter.
