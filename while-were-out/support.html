Support — While We're Out

For iPhone and iPad · requires iOS 17 or later

While We're Out makes the sheet you hand a babysitter, housesitter or petsitter: who to call, what the kids are allergic to, when the dog eats, where the water shut-off is. Answer the questions once and share a PDF.

Get in touch

Email becca@gebhardtechnology.com with a question, a bug, or a request. A real person reads it and will write back.

If you are reporting something going wrong, it helps to say which device you are on, which version of iOS, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I give the guide to a sitter?

Tap the share button at the top of the guide. The app builds a PDF and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet, so you can send it by Messages or email, AirDrop it, save it to Files, or print it for the fridge.

Do I have to reset anything between sitters?

No. Nothing in the guide belongs to a single visit — there is no sitter's name and there are no dates. It describes your house, so it is as true for the next sitter as for this one. Update anything that has changed and share it again.

Something I typed isn't showing up in the PDF.

Blank fields are deliberately skipped, so a half-filled guide still prints cleanly. If an entry is missing, it usually means the part that carries it is still empty — an entry under "Around the house", for instance, only prints once it has a location, instructions, or a photo. Check the section it belongs to and make sure the topic it sits under is switched on in Guide settings.

What is "Still to do"?

A list of the things a sitter most often asks for that you have not answered yet. Each row jumps straight to the screen that fixes it. It is a prompt, not a requirement — you can share the guide at any point.

We've moved. How do I start fresh?

Guide settings → Start over. It deletes the guide and its photos so you can answer the questions again for the new house. This cannot be undone.

Where is my information kept?

On your device only. The app has no account, no server, and no analytics, and makes no network connections. The only time anything leaves your device is when you share the PDF yourself. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Can I get the guide back if I delete the app?

Only from an Apple backup. Because the guide is stored on the device and nowhere else, deleting the app deletes it. If you are changing phones, restore from your iCloud or encrypted local backup and the guide comes with it. It is also worth keeping a shared copy of the PDF somewhere.
