Privacy Policy — While We're Out

Last updated 23 September 2026

While We're Out makes the guide you hand a babysitter, housesitter or petsitter. The short version of this policy: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. While We're Out has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The guide you write is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. That includes your address, the phone numbers you enter, notes about your children and your pets, and any photos you attach to entries such as the water shut-off or the breaker panel.

That data never leaves your device except as part of your own Apple backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup), which is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Only the PDF, and only when you send it. When you share or print the guide, iOS hands the file to whichever app or printer you choose, and what happens to it after that is governed by that app's own terms. We are not involved in that step and cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information on servers. We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • There are no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network connections.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only at the moment you add a photo to the guide, and only to put that photo into the guide. It does not read anything else from your library, and it does not ask for either permission at launch.

Information about children

The app is made for a parent or householder to use. It holds information about children — names, ages, allergies, medications, bedtimes — entered by an adult. That information is stored only on the device and leaves it only inside the PDF that the adult chooses to share. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it stored goes with it. Inside the app, Guide settings → Start over erases the guide and its photos without deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.
