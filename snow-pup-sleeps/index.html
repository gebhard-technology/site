Meet Oslo and Maui

Oslo and Maui are real dogs! Oslo is a German Shepherd/Husky mutt who lovingly watches over my kids, and Maui is his younger cousin, a fluffy Samoyed who's always the ringleader when the kids are playing. I made this countdown so my kids could count down to Christmas with their favorite pups, and now yours can too.

  • Count the sleepsThe home screen shows how many sleeps are left until Christmas.
  • A door every day25 illustrated scenes, one for each day from December 1 to Christmas morning.
  • Made for pre-readersToday's door is wrapped like a present and gently glows, so kids can find it without reading numbers.
  • No dead endsDoors that aren't ready show a sleeping puppy that wiggles when tapped. It never scolds.
  • Visit againOpened doors stay open, so favorite days can be seen again and again.
  • Works offlineNo internet needed. Everything is built into the app.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. The app works offline and everything stays on your device.
