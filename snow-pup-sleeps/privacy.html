Privacy Policy: Snow Pup Sleeps

Last updated 19 October 2026

Snow Pup Sleeps is a Christmas countdown for children. The short version of this policy: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Snow Pup Sleeps has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of information on your device: which doors have been opened this season and your grown-up settings, such as whether numbers are spoken out loud.

That information never leaves your device except as part of your own Apple backup, which is controlled entirely by your device settings and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • There are no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network connections.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Children's privacy

Snow Pup Sleeps is designed for families with young children. Because the app collects no data and makes no network connections, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

Links out of the app

The app contains no links to external websites, no social media features, and no way to contact anyone from inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.
