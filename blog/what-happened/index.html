Why I Quit My Job -- What Happened

By Becca Black · October 2026

Overview

I January 2026 I quit my job as a Principle Software Engineer.

I had my first baby in May of 2022. 23 months later I had my second. Each time I was fortunate that I got around 5 months of maternity leave but returning to work was hard.

  • Mom guilt – For me this hit so deep. This is a very personal thing for each mom. Personally, I simply felt like someone else (that I barely know) doing the majority of the parenting for my kids at such a young age felt deeply off. Dropping my kids off and having to leave them in tears crying no understanding what was going on was the hardest thing I’ve ever done in my life. It was so hard I honestly could not do it. My husband did drop off for my oldest because I’m not sure I would have lasted very long. I also was very fortunate that my boss let me work part time shortly after returning to work after having my son.
  • Breastfeeding – Ohhh man. I remember thinking this would be something that came so naturally in the classes I took. That's just getting started. It doesn't account for going back to work. I also had my first right in the mist of the 2022 formula shortage.
  • Sleep Deprivation - No explanation necessary. If your nursing you know this hits you as the woman much harder.
  • Sickness – They are constantly sick if they are in daycare. I mean constantly. I swear I missed like every other week of work due to illness.
  • Stack ranking – my peers at my job were all men. All of which either had no kids or had older kids (like in high school or college). 24/7 oncall and expectations of answering pings asap are just not my priority in my scarce free time as a mom with tiny kids.
