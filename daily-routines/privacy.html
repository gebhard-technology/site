Privacy Policy — Daily Routines

Last updated 14 September 2026

Daily Routines is a picture routine chart for children. The short version of this policy: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Daily Routines has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The app saves one file on your device containing your charts: the tasks you have chosen, which flaps are currently lifted, the date they were last reset, and each child's first name if you have entered one.

That file never leaves your device except as part of your own Apple backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup), which is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes the file.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information on servers. We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • There are no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network connections.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Children's privacy

Daily Routines is designed for families and is listed in the App Store Kids Category. Because the app collects no data and makes no network connections, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the App Store's Kids Category requirements by design.

A child's first name, if entered, is optional, is used only to display a message when a routine is finished, and is stored only on the device.

Links out of the app

The app contains no links to external websites, no social media features, and no way to contact anyone from inside the app.

Changes to this policy
