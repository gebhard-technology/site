Simple, calm apps for little kids

How I design

The short answer is that I design for my kids.

I take inspiration from the warmth and simplicity of Scandinavian design and the Montessori idea that children can do things for themselves when the tools fit their needs. I use gentle colors, clear pictures, and simple steps so kids can find their way and go at their own pace.

I want my apps to be fun and useful and not overstimulating or addictive.

I’m someone who prefers less stuff. There’s something nice about having a paper Advent calendar the kids can open themselves. As a mom of littles, I also get overwhelmed by how much there is to keep track of. When our routine chart lives on my phone or an old tablet, that’s one less thing to find a spot for at home or remember to pack when we leave. It’s also one less thing that might end up in a landfill someday.

What families can expect

  • No subscriptions. I prefer apps without a recurring bill, so that’s how I make mine.
  • No in-app purchases, logins, or accounts. They aren’t kid-friendly, so I leave them out.
  • No data collection. That one speaks for itself.
